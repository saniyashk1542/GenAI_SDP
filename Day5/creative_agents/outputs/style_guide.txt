**VP Brand Style Guide**

**1. Brand Summary**
The VP brand is built around providing exceptional interview experiences for college students, empowering them to showcase their skills and confidence to future employers. Our mission is to help young professionals stand out in a crowded job market. We strive to deliver high-quality resources and support that set us apart from the competition.

**2. Tone Words**
Empowering, authentic, supportive, insightful, aspirational

These tone words will guide all downstream communication, ensuring our messaging resonates with our target audience of college students.

**3. Voice Rules**

• Use a conversational tone that feels approachable and relatable to young adults.
• Avoid jargon and technical terms that might confuse or intimidate our audience.
• Use active voice whenever possible to convey confidence and authority.
• Incorporate storytelling techniques to make complex information more engaging and memorable.

**4. Messaging Pillars**

• **Unlock Your Potential**: Help college students discover their strengths, passions, and career aspirations.
• **Gain Confidence in the Interview Room**: Provide actionable tips, strategies, and resources to help young professionals feel prepared and confident.
• **Find Your Dream Job**: Inspire and support college students as they navigate the job market and find fulfilling careers that align with their values and goals.

**5. Do/Don't**

• **Do:** Use social media platforms like Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn to share engaging content, behind-the-scenes insights, and expert advice.
• **Do:** Collaborate with influencers, thought leaders, and industry experts to amplify our message and expand our reach.
• **Don't:** Use generic or template-based language that fails to capture the unique personality and tone of the VP brand.
• **Don't:** Overpromise or make unrealistic claims about our products or services – we're committed to delivering high-quality resources and support.
